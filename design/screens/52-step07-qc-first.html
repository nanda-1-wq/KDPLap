Quality Control

Next

CHECK THE WHOLE BOOK

Find problems before you format the book

Quality Control reads every section that has text. It lists what to fix and links each problem to its place in 06 Write. It never changes your text.

  • 1Complete. Sections not Final, empty, still "Needs review", or far under target.
  • 2Sources. "Verify" marks left in the text, and the sources named.
  • 3Repeats. The same sentence twice, and words used too often.
  • 4Pointers. "See Chapter 9" when there is no Chapter 9, and leftovers like [TODO].
  • 5Consistency. One word spelled two ways, US and UK spelling mixed.
  • 6Promise. Does each chapter keep the book's promise? AI · paid
  • 7KDP rules. Links, emoji, missing characters, length, disclaimer.
  • 8Result. Ready for Format, or ready with warnings.

Free. About 2 seconds. Runs groups 1 to 5 and 7.

6 · PROMISE CHECK · AI

Compares each chapter with your locked promise in 03 Positioning. One AI call per chapter. You see the cost before it runs.

GOOD TO KNOW

Never blocks. You can go to 08 Format at any time. Warnings are advice.

Fix in 06 Write. Each problem opens its section with the spot marked.

Not here: spelling and grammar. Use Word or Grammarly on the exported file.

Step 07 is done when the checks ran and the text has not changed since.
