The chapter title or objective, the section title, the word target, the other sections, the positioning lock, the pen name or its voice (the fingerprint, as today)Out of date

How the app knows (no migration): the check row keeps the version it read (0022 version_id) and its fingerprint. Each later version has a source (manual, generate, improve, expand, shorten, humanize, custom, restore). A used suggestion (0021) links to the version it made and keeps the passage it changed; it is "this check's Fix" when that passage holds the quote of one of this check's findings. The rule is one pure function, kdpFindingRules.stillValid(), tested case by case in Part 4.
