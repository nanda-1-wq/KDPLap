Fix states

Severity comes from the finding type, set by our code: drift and objective are major, voice is minor. "Keep as is" hides a finding, and it stays hidden on later checks when the type and the exact quote match. "Fix" opens Custom in Improve with the finding pre-filled. "Fix major" opens the major findings one at a time as normal suggestions. Code checks ("In your text") keep their look from 22c.
