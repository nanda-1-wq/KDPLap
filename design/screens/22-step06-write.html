4.2 Shoulder rolls, both ways

Shoulder rolls ease the stiffness that builds up after long hours of sitting. Studies show shoulder rolls cut neck pain by 40%.Verify: no source

Now lift your shoulders up toward your ears, then roll them back and down in a slow circle. Do this five times. Keep your neck long and your chin level. If anything pinches, make the circle smaller.

Next, reverse the direction: up, forward, and down. Sit near the front of your chair with both feet flat on the floor, and keep breathing slowly through your nose.
