## 3. Findings and "New, your call"

**F1. The design PNGs differ from the design's own HTML; the build follows the HTML.** In 59, 60 and 73c the PNG
shows the title in 2 lines and the IBM Plex Sans layers (subtitle, blurb, pen name) in a serif face. I opened
`design/screens/59-09-cover-text-fonts.html` itself in the test Chrome (`59-design-html-in-chrome.png`) and measured:
- the title is in 3 lines there too: "Micro Study Habits for" measures 364.8 px at 33.06 px Source Serif 4 Bold
  (Google Fonts), wider than its 360 px box; the title block is 121 px high (3 lines);
- the subtitle's computed font is "IBM Plex Sans", serif, and IBM Plex Sans is loaded: it draws in Plex.
So the PNGs come from the design tool's own renderer. The build's canvas (D10) draws what the HTML draws, with the
pinned TTFs the print PDF embeds (the same line measures 364 px there). Nothing to fix.
Your call only if you want the 2-line title of the PNG: the Band layout's title box would go from 360 to 370 px (a
change to the Part 1 layout tables).

**F3. The shell (S1 to S3).** The built shell is the approved 51b rail, as plan §3 says, not 58's own frame:
- S1: header 80 px (58: 72) and rail 76 px (58: 72);
- S2: the rail lists 01 to 09 with the save check at the bottom; 58 also shows 10 (open circle) and 11 (lock) and group lines;
- S3: Next has its chevron (58: plain text).
Rec: keep 51b (one shell for every wide step). Say if 09 should add the 10 and 11 rows.

**F4. The rail marks.**
- 02 and 06 show open circles: the cover mock book has no research rows or final sections. A gap in the mock, not
  a difference in the app (the 01 to 08 marks are the E14.3 shell, unchanged).
- 03 shows the lock: the shell's rule since E8 (a locked Positioning shows a lock, not a check). The 09 finals draw a check.
- 73: the design shows 08 done while 09 is Locked. Locked means there is no current Print PDF, and then 08 is not done,
  so the built rail shows 08 open. The design is inconsistent there; the build follows the data.

**R11. Two tabs (plan: decided at STOP 4).** When another tab saved first, the save updates 0 rows. Built: the Save failed
bar shows; Retry reads the row's rev again and writes this tab's design (last write wins). The other tab's change is lost
only if you Retry here. Rec: keep this for v2 (one user). Other option: Retry reloads the other tab's design instead (this
tab's last change lost).

**F5. A failed save while leaving 09.** The shell waits for 09's last save when you open another step (as 06 and 08).
If that save fails, the error stays in the sidebar's save line with Retry, which still saves the cover. If you open 09
again before that Retry, 09 loads the saved design (without the failed change), and a later Retry from the sidebar
would meet the new rev and fail again. Rare (one user, a failed network at that moment); noted, no change proposed.

**F6. Step D on the weight select.** A `<select>` cannot have an `::after` click area. Built: the select is 44 px high
and clear, its 36 px box is drawn by a wrapper, so the look is the design's. Nothing to decide.
