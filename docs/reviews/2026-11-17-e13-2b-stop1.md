### Page counts and KDP printing cost (owner request)

Cost source: KDP Help, "Paperback Printing Cost" (kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834340, read Nov 17, 2026). Amazon.com, black ink, white or cream paper:
- 24 to 110 pages: a fixed **$2.30** (regular trim) or **$2.84** (large trim: wider than 6.12 in or taller than 9 in).
- 110 to 828 pages: **$1.00 + $0.012 a page** (regular) or **$1.00 + $0.017 a page** (large).
- The cost is per printed copy, before royalty.

## 4. Your decision (Q1)

1. **A as planned (my recommendation for 8.5 × 11).** Readable lines (66 to 77 characters), the same look as 6 × 9. Cost: +$0.06 to $0.13 a copy over B at 30,000 words. B also breaks the 10% words-per-page rule at 8.5 × 11.
2. **A for 8.5 × 11, but a wider text block for 8 × 10** (for example 6.0 in: inside 0.875, outside 1.125). The likely result is about 72 to 81 characters a line and about 10% fewer pages than 6 × 9. That would cut part of the $0.67. I have not built this yet: if you want it, I run it as a second proof before Part 2.
3. **B (today's margins on large trims).** Cheapest (fewest pages), but 91 to 114 characters a line, and 8.5 × 11 fails the 10% rule.
4. Your own numbers: I run them as a second proof.

My recommendation: **A for 8.5 × 11.** For 8 × 10, either keep A or ask for option 2. The cost gap matters most on 8 × 10.
