## 3 · The busy line (Q-1, a planned difference to design 63)
**Flow.** A Pixabay pick answers `stock_busy` with `retry_after_s` N (1 to 120):
1. The error slot shows "Pixabay is busy. Trying again in N s." as `<p class="cv-uperr" role="status">` with design 63's warning icon. In the grid the class is `cv-uperr cv-sd-err`, the same as today's pick error there.
2. Each second only the number changes, in place (no redraw). The visible words are `aria-hidden`. A `.sr-only` copy with the first wording sits in the `role="status"` paragraph and never changes. So a screen reader says it once.
3. At 0 the same pick runs once more by itself (`pick(E, item, true)`). Success: the picture goes in, as after any pick. A second failure shows that code's message: for `stock_busy` that is design 63's "Pixabay is not answering right now. Try again in a minute, or use your own uploads." There is no second wait.
4. **Cancel:** picking any picture (another, or the same one again: it is then picked at once), closing the picker (×, Escape, the gray around it), or leaving 09 (`E.live()` is false; the timer stops itself).
5. **stock v1** (no `retry_after_s`), or a bad value (0, 121, 2.5, "20", -5, null): today's message at once, no wait.
6. **Unsplash** `stock_busy`: its message at once (the line says "Pixabay"; stock v2 only sends `retry_after_s` on Pixabay picks).
7. While it waits, the tiles and "Add to cover" stay on. That is the "pick another picture" path. "Adding the picture…" does not show.

**`t-stock-retry.js` (32 checks):**
- the grid: the words, same class, color, size and icon as the error slot, `role="status"`, the countdown, the status text unchanged after a tick, no call while counting, the same pick sent once more (identical body), the picture added;
- a second failure: design 63's message (an alert), exactly 2 calls;
- stock v1 and 4 bad values: today's message, one call, no wait;
- the preview's slot; Add to cover stays on; Back to results moves the line to the grid's slot and the wait goes on;
- another picture cancels; the same picture again picks at once, with no extra call after;
- × and Escape cancel (no retry, no line left in the panel);
- the panel's slot, same style, the retry works;
- Unsplash: no wait, no retry;
- leaving 09 for 08: no retry.

## 6 · Known limits (not fixed; your word)
- **L1, "said once" on a full redraw.** The countdown number changes in place, so ticks are silent. But a redraw of the whole picker or panel during the wait creates the `role="status"` paragraph again: switching the source tab, Back to results, opening the preview, or a search finishing. Some screen readers may then read the line again (with its first number). The same holds for every status and alert line in 09 today. A fix would need one live region outside the redrawn HTML. I left it, as the plan did not ask for that.
- **L2, the retry after other actions.** The automatic retry still runs after a tab switch, a new search or a preview of another picture. Only the 3 cancel rules stop it. That follows the plan. Say if any of these should cancel too.
