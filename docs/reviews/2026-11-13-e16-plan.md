## 1. Architecture

- **One model.** A cover = `{ fmt: {trim, pages, paper}, bg, layers[], layout }`.
  - Layers keep the design's shapes exactly (§1 of the design reader).
  - Board px at 70 px per inch, origin at the top left of the full wrap with bleed.
  - `size`/`sw`/`bw` in pt. z-order = array order. Text has no stored height.
- **One text layout.** `js/cover/textlayout.js` breaks lines with a `measure(font, weight, size, text)` function.
  - In the browser that is `ctx.measureText` on the loaded TTF; in Deno tests it is a fixed-width stub.
  - Heights for checks, remap and handles all come from it. This replaces the design's `offsetHeight` and its estimate.
- **One raster renderer.** `js/cover/render.js` paints a cover at any scale: the screen (zoom), flatten patches and the full image (300 DPI), Kindle (1600 × 2560), thumbnails.
  - Effects with canvas calls. Distortion with a mesh warp.
  - Picture filters with our own pixel code (Safari's `ctx.filter` is not reliable).
  - Tiles for big sizes (R5).
- **One Typst writer.** `js/cover/typst.js` turns the model plus a flatten plan into Typst source.
  - Vector text uses the same line breaks.
  - Shapes and icons become Typst `curve` paths.
  - Raster parts become opaque JPEG patches on a global 300 DPI grid, so seams line up.
- **Flatten plan** (`js/cover/flatten.js`, pure). The B2 change (Nov 13 09:30), S4b and S6 say what is flattened:
  - opacity under 100;
  - a transparent PNG;
  - an image of any kind (crop, filters, darken, mask, border and flip are baked into the picture);
  - glow, lift, distortion;
  - a shadow or background effect with strength under 100 or softness over 0;
  - a gradient fill, if Part 2 shows Typst gradients break the one-sRGB rule (R7).
- **What a patch holds:** only the raster part and what is under it, rendered opaque at 300 DPI.
  - For a text effect (shadow, glow, lift, background), the patch is the effect alone; the letters stay vector on top.
  - The letters become raster only when the text's opacity is under 100 or distortion is on.
  - Images, see-through shapes and transparent PNGs are rasterized whole.
  - The patch box grows to hold any whole vector layer under it that it cuts, so no text is split. Layers above stay vector.
- **Engine:** Typst 0.14.2 via typst.ts 0.7.0, with the 08 pins (`js/format/files.js`).
  - From E15 (Q-1): `engine.buildSource({kind:'cover'})` and `mapShadow` for images.
  - PDF checks (E15 `coverChecks`): one page; size within 0.001 in; fonts embedded; exactly one ICCBased sRGB profile; no OutputIntents, transparency or SMask.

## 3. Editor (B)

The editor is ported from the design reader's report. Each file lives under `js/cover/`. Styles go in a new `css/cover.css`, tokens only. Canvas-only colors become `--cv-*` tokens, defined once in `css/cover.css`: selection blue, guide green, fold blue, snap magenta, KDP pink and yellow. **Model and history** (`model.js`, `history.js`):
- `normalize()` fills defaults (`FX_DEF`, `common()`) and drops unknown fields on load.
- Undo:
  - 50 steps, with a 500 ms sliding window (a drag or a slider = one step);
  - captures `{layers, bg}` only;
  - keys Cmd/Ctrl+Z, Shift+Cmd+Z and Ctrl+Y;
  - history resets on a size change.

**Canvas view** (`view.js`, our renderer + DOM overlay):
- Zoom, Fit (whole wrap), pinch, two-finger pan, drag on gray.
- The KDP template layer:
  - drawn from trim, pages and paper;
  - transparency 0 to 100, and hide;
  - never exported; its words stay as drawn (Nov 13 19:42).
- Guide lines. Snap: the front center within 6 px/zoom; rotation every 45° within 4°.
- Handles:
  - text: width (min 60) + rotate;
  - image: 4 corners that keep the shape (min 30);
  - shape: 8 handles (min 10); a line has 2 ends.
- Select:
  - Shift- or Cmd-click, box select, Cmd+A, Esc;
  - arrows nudge 1 px, 10 px with Shift; the Delete key deletes.
- Forward and backward jump past the next overlapping layer.

**Text** (`text.js` and the context bar):
- Presets 36, 20 and 12 pt; "From your book".
- Font list:
  - search and 5 groups; names drawn from a sprite (§8);
  - weights only where the family has the file (D5);
  - size steps as in the design (6 to 160).
- Color, align, capitals (`ls` 0.12). In-place editing uses a DOM text box over the canvas while typing.
- Effects:
  - 9 styles, with the per-style controls and ranges;

**Images** (`images.js`):

## 4. Size changes from 08 (C)

Size comes from three places: trim from the Brief, pages from the current Print PDF, paper from `book_formats` (default white). Each one triggers the remap differently:
- **Trim** is in `format_key` (0027:353). A trim change stales the Print PDF, so 09 shows Locked until a new PDF exists. Remap on the next open.
- **Pages** change only with a new Print PDF. Remap on the next open.
- **Paper** is not in `format_key`. The PDF stays current and 09 stays open, but the spine changes. Remap at once on open.

**Rule:** on every 09 open (and on return to step 09 in the shell), compare `cover_designs.fmt` with the current fmt. If they differ:
1. Run the design's `remap(layers, A, B)` unchanged.
2. Save with the new fmt and `last_remap`, and write a history snapshot.
3. Reset undo and run Fit.

**What the user sees:**
- The amber note (F72) with its exact words, "Show what moved", and "Add spine text" when spine text is newly allowed.
- The header "The book size changed: export again."
- Next turns off. Exports go stale through the key (fmt is in `cover_key`).
- The note stays until it is closed or until the next export (`last_remap.seen`).

**Ported as is:**
- Image crops reset on remap (`coverFit`).
- Spine text stays when the page count drops; C5 then shows it as Must fix, as F72 shows.
- "Moved" counts only x, y and w changes.

## 5. Export (D)

The pipeline (`export.js`) follows the design's step list exactly.

1. **Print cover PDF** (always):
   - Typst from the layers and the flatten plan.
   - Size `0.25 + 2·trim + spine` × `trim + 0.25`.
   - Only the fonts in use are embedded.
   - Patches are opaque JPEGs at 300 DPI; one sRGB profile, as KDP accepted (qa-A, D, E, F).
2. **KDP rule check:**
   - `coverChecks` on the PDF bytes.
   - Each font in use has every glyph of its text (`font-cmap.js`). A miss stops this step and names the font and the letter (D9).
   - Typst line widths are compared with the screen layout (E15 fit markers) and must match within 0.5%.
3. **Full image** (on by default): PNG or JPG at 300 DPI from the renderer, without the template, hidden or excluded layers.
4. **Kindle JPG** (toggle, on by default):
   - 1600 × 2560, from the front cut at the trim to 1 : 1.6.
   - kFit (wide trims, where the cut is over 0.375 in): the front is scaled to width, with the background color above and below.
   - The file is read back with `jpegColor`: 3 channels, no Adobe marker, sRGB or no profile.
5. **Save to My layouts** (Q-3 a, only on export): layers, fmt, bg and thumb. This replaces the entry for this book (Q-3b).

After the 5 steps:
- One `cover_exports` row per kind (print, image, kindle). Sizes are stored to 4 decimals and shown to 3.
- File names: `<title-slug>_<trim>_<pages>p_<paper>_cover.pdf|png|jpg` and `_kindle.jpg`.
- Download per file, or "Download all (.zip)" from our own store-only zip writer (no library).
- The KDP "where it goes" lines. The header reads "Done: cover files exported." and Next turns on.

**Blocking and failures:**
- Any Must fix blocks export, and so does being offline.
- Stop works between steps. A stop or a failure records nothing.

**Unsplash pixels:** they are fetched in the browser from the hotlink URL (`images.unsplash.com` sends `access-control-allow-origin: *`, checked Nov 13). The exported cover is a derivative image, and Unsplash allows that without hotlinking.

## 7. Stock images (F)

A new Edge Function, `supabase/functions/stock/`, in the same layout as `generate`:
- `lib.ts` (pure: validate, normalize, cache key, credit);
- `handler.ts` (deps injected);
- `index.ts` (Deno.serve, supabase-js with the **user's JWT**, and the same CORS list as generate).

**Keys:**
- `PIXABAY_API_KEY` and `UNSPLASH_ACCESS_KEY`, as Supabase secrets only.
- The owner types them straight into the Supabase dashboard (Edge Functions, Secrets): never in code, chats or git.
- A missing key returns `not_configured`, and the panel shows the stock-down message.

**Input:**
- `{action:'search', source, q (1 to 100 chars), page 1 to 20, filters}`.

  - Unsplash filters: color, `content_filter=high`.
- `{action:'pick', source, id}`.
- The body is at most 2 KB, every field is checked, and errors have clear codes.

**Search:**
- A per-user cache hit under 24 h answers at once; otherwise the function calls the provider.
- Results are normalized to `{id, source, thumb, preview, width, height, type, transparent, author, authorUrl, pageUrl}`, 24 per page.
- Every result list shows its source (Pixabay rule).

**Pick, Pixabay:**
- The server looks the image up **by id** at Pixabay. It never fetches a URL sent by the browser.
- It downloads `largeImageURL` (at most 1,280 px), or `imageURL` once full API access is granted (env `PIXABAY_FULL=1`).
- At most 15 MB; the type is checked by magic bytes.
- It stores the file at `covers/<uid>/assets/<uuid>.jpg|png` (no permanent hotlinking), inserts a `cover_assets` row, and the picture then shows in Uploads.

**Pick, Unsplash:**
- The server gets `/photos/:id`, then calls `links.download_location` (the download ping).
- It inserts a `cover_assets` row with the hotlink `urls.raw` and makes no copy (Unsplash rule 1). Uploads shows it as a linked picture.
- The credit "Photo by [name] on Unsplash" links both names with `?utm_source=nicheink&utm_medium=referral`. It shows in the picker, the Images panel and the layer's More menu.

**Rate limits (per user in v2):**
- 30 searches and 10 picks a minute.
- Unsplash demo: 50 calls an hour for the whole app. With one user in v2, the hourly budget env `STOCK_UNSPLASH_HOURLY=45` covers it.
- A provider 429 returns `stock_busy`. A 10 s timeout returns `stock_unavailable`.

**Error state:** the design's "[Pixabay] is not answering right now. Try again in a minute, or use your own uploads." in the panel and in the large view.

**Picture quality:**
- Without full API access, Pixabay gives at most 1,280 px.
  - On a 6 in front that is about 213 DPI (amber).
  - On a full 6 × 9 wrap it is about 103 DPI (red, Must fix).
  - The preview's "Sharp up to W × H in at 300 DPI" says so honestly.
- Unsplash `raw` has the full size.
