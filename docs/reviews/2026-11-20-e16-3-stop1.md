## 2 · Behaviour, before and after (one Pixabay pick; 2 retries shared, 20 s of waiting, 45 s deadline, all unchanged)
| What Pixabay sends with a 429 | stock v2 (live) | stock v3 |
|---|---|---|
| `Retry-After: 0` (the live answer, Cloudflare, Nov 20) | waits 1 s (`MIN_WAIT_MS`), 1 s; busy → `retry_after_s: 1` | **waits 1.5–2.5 s, then 4–6 s; busy → `retry_after_s: 20`** |
| `X-RateLimit-Reset: 0` | the same as above (1 s, 1 s; 1) | **as no header** |
| `Retry-After: 1` or `1.5`, or an HTTP-date 1 s ahead | waits 1 s or 1.5 s; busy → 1 or 2 | **as no header** |
| `Retry-After: 2` | waits 2 s, 2 s; busy → 2 | **unchanged**: 2 s, 2 s; busy → 2 |
| `Retry-After: 3` to `20` | waited | unchanged |
| over the wait budget (`25`, `500`) | not waited; busy → 25, 120 | unchanged |
| no header | 1.5–2.5 s, then 4–6 s; busy → 20 | unchanged |

**The log line** still prints the raw header: `retry-after 0`, so the logs show what Pixabay sent; the `wait` field shows the backoff actually taken. Example from the tests:
`stock: pixabay download HTTP 429 (try 1 of 3, hop 0 pixabay.com, server cloudflare, cf-mitigated -, type text, bytes 17, wait 1.5 s, retry-after 0, reset -, remaining -)`

**The browser (Part 3 of E16.2):** a busy pick that ends with a 0 header now gets `retry_after_s: 20`. So the panel counts down 20 s (not 1 s) before its one automatic retry. That gives Pixabay's window time to pass, as on Nov 18 (a retry after 84 s and 231 s worked).
