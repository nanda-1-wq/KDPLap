## 4. generate v28 (`cover_blurb` v2)

**Request:** `{ stage: "cover_blurb", bookId, length: short|medium|long, tone: warm|direct|expert, authorLine: true|false }`, exactly these keys, at most 2 KB.
- v27's `{ stage, bookId }` is now `bad_request`. Nothing on main or live calls it (main's 09 shows "Coming next").

**Reply:** `{ stage, blurb, words, unsourced }`. No headline.

**Context:** the server reads the pen name's `bio_text` with the title, subtitle, pen name and locked Positioning, through RLS. The browser never sends the bio. Nothing from the Brief or Research (C8).

**Prompt:**
- System: the word target comes from the last line of the user message. The tone rules are fixed:
  - warm: friendly and encouraging;
  - direct: short, plain sentences;
  - expert: calm and precise, never claiming credentials.
- `author_line`: one sentence of at most 30 words. It starts with the pen name and uses only facts stated in `<bio>`.
- The rule "Do not describe the author" now reads: "Do not describe the author in the blurb. Facts about the author go only in author_line, and only from <bio>."
- The bio is in its own escaped `<bio>` tag, sent only with the author line, cut at a word to 1,500 characters.
- The schema has `author_line` only when it is asked for.
- Last line, for example: "Write the blurb in about 130 words (115 to 145), in the warm tone, and the author line, following the rules."

**Our code decides what is kept:**
- The author line is cleaned to one plain line and joined as the last paragraph. It is dropped when not asked for.
- The whole text must be 1 to 1,500 characters (0029), or the call fails and is not counted. This is tested with Long + the author line at 1,501.
- **Number rule:** the blurb's numbers need the Positioning, title or subtitle. The author line's numbers may also come from the bio (§7, N5).

**Checks, in order:** positioning locked → title → bio when the author line is asked for. Same model (Sonnet 5.5), 800 tokens, 60 s.
