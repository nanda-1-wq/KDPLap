## 4. Findings and "New, your call"

### Findings (no decision needed)

**F1. Design 61: the design's own tile row is squeezed, not scrolled.** I opened `design/screens/61-09-cover-text-effects.html` in the
test Chrome (`61-design-html-in-chrome.png`). Its Text effects popover is not scrolled (scrollTop 0), but its style-tile row
is 4 px high (each tile 16 px). The popover is a column with a 640 px height cap, and the row is allowed to shrink. That is
why the PNG shows all four shadow sliders under the color. The build keeps the 9 tiles whole (they are the main choice), so
at a 900 px window Strength shows and Distance, Direction and Softness are one scroll down. The slider rows use the design's
28 px pitch. Your call only if you want the design's exact view: then the tiles must shrink to about 40 px high.

**F2. Design 66: the ⋮ menu's place in the final is set by hand.** The final opens the menu at x 414, y 150 (`applyFinal` F66).
A real click uses the design's own `openMenu` rule: the button's right edge + 22 px, beside its row. The build follows that
rule, and the compare shot uses the final's numbers.

**F3. A Part 4 bug, found and fixed: a pan or a resize while typing in place ended the typing.** Drawing the overlay again
replaced the text box, its blur ended the typing, and the next letters were lost. A two-finger pan while typing does this.
It most likely explains the one failed run of `t-text.js` in the first full pass (3 checks: the letters did not reach the layer,
Escape did not end, focus still in the box), where a bar's height change set off a redraw. The overlay now keeps the same
text box. `t-text.js` has a new check (pan + resize, then type); it passed 3 times alone and in the final run.

**F4. A Part 4 gap, fixed: the picker lost the keyboard focus.** After picking a swatch or typing a hex, focus went back to
the page. The picker's controls now carry keys, so focus comes back (new check in `t-shapes.js`).

**F5. Icons in the print file.** Tabler draws each path on its own with the non-zero rule; our shapes use even-odd (for the
frame and ring holes). Icons keep Tabler's rule, one Typst curve per path. Every icon was checked against the browser's own
drawing of Tabler's SVG (§2). A border on an icon outlines each path on its own; where two paths of one icon meet, both
outlines show. The paw (5 paths, bordered) is on the QA sheet and in the icons shot.

**F7. The panel column could squeeze its parts.** The first icons shot showed the icon search field squeezed to about 18 px (the
same flex rule as F1). The panel's parts no longer shrink; the panel scrolls instead. Fixed before the final shots.

**F6. File layout.** The plan named `js/cover/icons.js`. The icon tiles live in `js/cover/shapes-ui.js`, because they are part
of the Shapes panel. The set itself is `js/cover/icon-set.js`, as planned.
