### Item 4 · Replace takes the new picture's name
- **The repro test** (`cover t-replace-name.js`) ran before the fix. It covered 5 paths (a new file upload, an Uploads tile, a PDF page, a Pixabay pick, an Unsplash pick), each on a layer named by its picture and on a layer with a typed name "man".
  - With a picture name, all 5 paths already took the new name.
  - **With a typed name, all 5 kept "man"**, because a name typed in Layers (`label`) wins in `names.layerName`. That is your bug.
  - A 2nd finding: a Replace made less than 0.5 s after another change joined that change's Undo step (the history's 0.5 s window), so one Undo went back 2 steps.
- **Fix** (`js/cover/images.js` `place()`): Replace drops the typed name and is its own Undo step (`hist.step()` before and after, as a layout is). One Undo brings back the old picture and its old name.
