### 2.2 · What it says about the cause (one sample; the log adds the detail)
- **Part 1 works live.** The busy number is **20**, not 1. So the pick got no usable header (a `Retry-After` under 2 s
  now counts as none), and the server took the long backoff. The 6.7 s from the reserve to the release fits two no-header
  waits (1.5 to 2.5 s, then 4 to 6 s) plus three short tries. At E16.2 the same failure took 3.5 s with 1 s waits.
- **Part 2 works live.** The preview showed the tile's `_640` file. Opening it made no request, and nothing in the
  visit loaded a `_1280` file.
- **But the pick after the preview still failed.** At E16.2, H1 was read as "the browser's `_1280` load on our key's
  link trips a per-key limit on `/get/` just before the server's download". **This sample goes against that reading:**
  no `_1280` load happened at all, and the pick still failed.
- What the browser did load on our key's links: **24 `_640` files** (17:10:22Z to about 17:10:50Z), 1 to 1.5 minutes
  before the pick. At E16.2 the tile pick worked 1.6 s after the same 24 loads, so those loads alone do not explain it either.
- One sample cannot tell the causes apart. The log can: if the 3 failures are again **download, HTTP 429, hop 0,
  pixabay.com, server cloudflare**, the limit sits on the server's `/get/` download, not on anything the browser does.
  Open ideas for after the log (not tested, no change made):
  (a) Pixabay or Cloudflare limits `/get/` downloads from the function's data-centre address, whatever the browser does;
  (b) the limit depends on the picture (7744408 is the same picture the server downloaded at E16.2 pick 1, Nov 20, 200);
  (c) it fails on the lookup instead (then it is the API limit, and the log shows `lookup HTTP 429`).
