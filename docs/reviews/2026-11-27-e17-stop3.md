## What was built
- **N1** Right panel hidden by default. One 44 px toggle `[data-panel-toggle]` in the top right of the writing area,
  in its own grid cell (never over a control). `aria-pressed`, `aria-controls="wrSide"`, mist fill + ink border when open.
  Stored in `localStorage` `nicheink.write.panel` ("open"/"closed"), read once in `init` inside try/catch (blocked = closed;
  a blocked write keeps the state for the visit). Escape while focus is in the panel hides it and focuses the toggle.
  The amber count shows only while the panel is hidden: `--warning` fill, `--surface` number (as design 78b), the number
  `aria-hidden`; name and title "Show versions, checks and Brief, N check(s) to look at". "Run AI check" in the Needs
  review note opens the panel. 900 to 1200: the open panel sits under the editor, as before. CLAUDE.md §7 exception added.
- **N2** Compact head: `.wr-top` grid; one row from a main width of 60rem (container query, so 200% text wraps).
  "Every section in this chapter has text." is no longer a line: the grey Generate chapter has `aria-disabled` (focusable,
  click and Enter do nothing), the words as `title` and as a hidden `aria-describedby` target. The other reasons and the
  "Writes the N sections…" line stay as a line under the head (plan named only the "full" one).
  "Select text to improve it." is a span inside the toolbar after Improve. Spacing: main top 16, head to card 12 (empty
  gen/imp boxes no longer add a gap), editor top 24. `--app-wr-measure: 680px` in css/app.css; the editor, the stream and
  the hint share one centered column.
- **N3** Header Export hidden (`hidden`) on 01 to 07. The 08 Word row reads "Word (.docx) or Markdown (.md)".
