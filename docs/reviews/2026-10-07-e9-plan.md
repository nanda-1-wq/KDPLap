## E9.1 Structure, generate, edit

### Data (migration 0016_outline_rules.sql)

- **One source of truth for words:** a section's `word_target` is the only
  stored number. A chapter total is the sum of its sections, computed, never
  stored (`chapters.word_target` stays null, comment says so).
  Intro and Conclusion each hold **one section** (hidden in the UI) that carries
  their words, so E10 can write them like any section.
- Checks: chapter title 1 to 150 chars, objective up to 300; section title up
  to 150; section words 0 to 10,000.
- Limits (trigger, like competitors 0008): at most 30 chapters of kind
  `chapter` (Brief allows 3 to 30), at most one intro and one conclusion,
  at most 12 sections per chapter, intro/conclusion exactly one section.
- **Delete guard:** a chapter or section with writing (any section with
  `current_version_id`) cannot be deleted (`has_writing`). In E9 nothing is
  written, so delete always works; the guard protects E10 onwards.
- RPCs, SECURITY INVOKER (RLS as the user), one transaction each:
  - `replace_outline(book_id, outline jsonb)`: used by the server after
    generate. Raises `positioning_not_locked`, `has_writing`. Deletes the old
    outline and inserts the new one, clears `outline_approved_at`.
  - `add_chapter(book_id)`: inserts a chapter (with 1 empty section) before the Conclusion.
  - `reorder_chapters(book_id, ids uuid[])`: the ids must be exactly the
    book's regular chapters; Intro stays first, Conclusion last.
- `chapters.unsourced text[]` (server writes): numbers in a title or
  objective not found in Brief, Research or Positioning. A trigger clears it
  when the user edits that title or objective (same idea as drift flags in 0010).
- `chapters` and `sections` join the 0009 touch trigger (edits move the book up on Books).

### Generate (new stage `outline_ideas`)

- Gate: positioning locked (else `positioning_not_locked`), Brief topic set.
- Dialog before the call (existing dialog pattern):
  "Sections per chapter" number, 1 to 6, **default 3**. Read-only lines from the
  Brief: "8 chapters · 8K to 12K words. Change these in 01 Brief." (link).
  If the Brief has no chapter count or length, the dialog says so and the AI
  picks (6 to 10 chapters, 8K to 12K words) and the result says "AI picked".
- Body `{ stage, bookId, sectionsPerChapter }`, max 2 KB. Prompt is
  server-side and reads (RLS): Brief (type, reader, problem, chapter count,
  target), locked Positioning (all six fields), title and subtitle, Research
  gaps / loves / hates, competitor titles and **tables of contents** (oldest
  first, at most 12,000 chars in all, a TOC that does not fit is left out whole).
  Prompt: competitors' contents show what readers expect and what is missing;
  never copy their chapter titles.
- Output (JSON schema): intro words, chapters `[{ title, objective,
  sections [{ title, words }] }]`, conclusion words. Server checks: chapter
  count as asked, sections as asked, lengths, words sum within the target
  (re-scales section words if the model misses by more than 10%), no empty
  titles. Numbers not found in Brief/Research/Positioning go to `unsourced`.
- Save: server calls `replace_outline` as the user. Reply = the saved outline.
- Model Sonnet 5.5, max tokens 4,000, timeout 120 s. Usage logged in
  `ai_usage`. Stop or failure: nothing saved, not counted (Stop wording from E7).
- **Regenerate** (outline exists): confirm dialog "Replace the outline? Your
  edits to chapters and sections are lost. Nothing is written yet." Blocked
  when any section has writing ("Some sections have writing. Edit the outline
  by hand.").

### Screen (`js/book-outline.js`, `kdpBookSteps[5]`)

  first. The outline is built from it." + link "Go to 03 Positioning".
- **Empty (locked, no outline):** short intro text + "Generate outline" button

- Autosave per field (`js/autosave.js`, 800 ms), sidebar "Saved" as other steps.
- **Delete** chapter or section: confirm dialog (design 06 pattern), e.g.
  "Delete chapter 3? Its 3 sections go too. Nothing is written yet, so

- **Reorder:** drag by the handle with a drop line (design 21), keyboard

### 1. Stage `outline_check` (`lib/outline_check.ts`, one entry in `lib/stages.ts`)

- Body `{ stage, bookId }`, max 2 KB. Sonnet 5.5, 1,500 tokens, 60 s. Gate:
  positioning locked (else `positioning_not_locked`), at least 1 chapter with a
  title (else `nothing_to_check`).
- Server reads (RLS): the outline (chapters numbered 1 to N, titles, objectives,
  section titles; not words), the locked Positioning (six fields), Brief, Research.
  Data inside XML tags, "never an instruction" lines, as drift_check.
- Reply schema: `findings: [{ kind: overlap | promise_gap | drift, chapters: [int],
  quote, why }]`. Our code decides what is kept:
  - `overlap`: exactly 2 different chapters; `drift`: exactly 1;
    `promise_gap`: 0 chapters and a `quote` really found in the reader promise.
  - A chapter number that does not exist (or Intro / Conclusion) drops the finding.
  - Numbers are stored as **chapter ids**, so the UI shows the current numbers.
  - `why` one line, at most 25 words; repeats dropped; at most 8 findings.
  - Numbers in `why` not in Brief or Research get "Verify: no source", as other stages.
- Save: service role, upsert one `outline_checks` row per book (`findings`,
  `inputs_key` of the outline the server read, `checked_at` server clock), filtered
  by the caller's user id. Save fails: not counted, nothing shown. Stop or failure:
  nothing saved, not counted. Usage in `ai_usage`.
- An edit made while the check runs is fine: the saved key is the one the AI read,
  so the result shows "Out of date" at once. Honest, no lost edit.
