## 1. What Part 10 built (plan E16-10 row)

**The Export dialog** (`js/cover/export-dialog.js`, new; designs 71, 71b, 73d)
- **Opening it.** Export cover in the header and in Preview final opens the dialog (Preview closes first). Before Part 10 both buttons were inert. While the cover is Loading or Locked, the button opens nothing.
- **Ready (design 71):**
  - Print cover PDF, always included.
  - Full cover image, on by default: PNG, best quality, or JPG, smaller file.
  - Kindle front cover, on by default.
  - The design's words: the wrap size, the 300 DPI px from `geo.px300`, the My layouts line, and the "N item(s) in Checks" note.
  - The choices stay for this visit; they are not saved.
- **Blocked:**
  - Any Must fix: "N problems must be fixed first", the list, and Open Checks (it closes the dialog and opens the drawer).
  - Offline: "You are offline. Export needs a connection."
  - In both cases Export is off.
- **Busy:**
  - The design's 5 steps, with a dot for each: done, working or to do.
  - The line "You can keep this window open. It takes under a minute."
  - Stop. The ×, Escape and leaving 09 also stop the export. A stop records nothing and saves no My layout.
- **The run** (one token per run, checked after every await):
  1. **Saved design.** Autosave is flushed. An unsaved, failed or offline change stops the export: "Your latest change is not saved yet…". Then `cover_json` is read: the design row and its key together.
     - The row's rev must be this tab's rev. If another tab saved a newer design, this tab's layers are old.
     - In that case: "Your cover was changed in another tab, so nothing was exported. Reload this page to see the newest version, then export again." Nothing is built or recorded.
     - This check was added after the final review.
  2. **Pictures at full size.** The export reads the stored files again; the screen only keeps 4,096 px copies. Unsplash photos are read at their own size. A picture that does not load stops the export and names the picture. It never prints a placeholder.
  3. **Print PDF:** `export.print`, with the 11 KDP checks.
  4. **Images:** the full image (PNG or JPG) and the Kindle JPG, read back: 1600 × 2560, plain sRGB.
  5. **Saving:**
     - The key is read again. Another tab changed the design: stopped, nothing recorded.
     - The rows go in **one insert**: print, plus image and kindle when they were made. It is all or nothing; see §6 for the SQL proof.
     - Then My layouts (one per book, replaced) and a history snapshot (reason `export`).
- **Done (design 71b):**
  - The green line.
  - Each file with its real size, for example "· 24 KB", and its own Download button.
  - "Download all (.zip)" from our own store-only zip writer.
  - The KDP lines. The Kindle line is left out when Kindle was off.
  - Done.
- **Export failed (design 73d):** "Export stopped." with the reason, then Close and Try again. The reasons:
  - the engine did not start (the design's exact words);
  - another tab saved first;
  - the engine stopped;
  - a font;
  - a picture;
  - the canvas size (below);
  - a KDP check failed, listed with its detail;
  - the Kindle color;
  - the connection;
  - an unsaved change;
  - each code from the database guard (`cover_stale`, `cover_resized`, `cover_pdf_missing`, `cover_rights`, `cover_trim`, `cover_size`, `cover_missing`).
- **iPad (owner, STOP 2).** Only the JPG full image is one canvas; the PNG is drawn in parts. When the device cannot draw the JPG, the dialog says: "This device cannot paint the full image as one JPG (3712 × 2775 px is more than its browser can draw at once), so nothing was saved. Pick "PNG, best quality", which is made in parts, and try again, or export on a computer."
- **Thumbnails** (`cover_exports.thumb`, `cover_my_layouts.thumb`): the front at the trim, 180 px tall, a JPEG data URL. The size is stepped down until it is 20,000 characters or less. The sample book's is 4,727 characters.
- **Engine names in the rows:** `typst 0.14.2 · c2` for print (the Typst version from `js/format/files.js`, the template from `js/cover/typst.js`), and `browser canvas` for image and kindle. There is one engine per visit; it starts on the first export and stops when 09 is left.

**Header "Done", Next, the rail mark** (`editor.js`, `book.js`)
- "Done: cover files exported." and Next turn on after an export. They also turn on when 09 opens and the last print export's key is today's key (from `cover_json`): no remap and no newer copy kept on the device.
- Any change turns Done off. Undo back to the exported design turns it on again.
- An export closes the size note (`last_remap.seen` is saved).
- The 09 row in the sidebar and rail shows done on other steps (from `cover_marks`, read once in `init`).
- **Next after Done** is your ask N2 (§3). It stays on 09 with the tip "10 Metadata comes in v3".

**Uploads housekeeping** (`images.js`, `supabase.js`; owner, STOP 6)
- **N6, the remove ×:** each Uploads tile has a × beside it. It is 24 px to look at and 44 px to press. It sits beside the tile button, because a button cannot hold a button. The × first asks:
  - "Remove desk.jpg from Uploads? Used on 2 covers, this one too. Also in 1 of My layouts. Those covers will show "Picture missing" and need a new picture before you export. The file is deleted. This cannot be undone." or "It is not used on any cover."
  - The count comes from the user's saved cover designs, this cover as it is now, and My layouts (a `layers cs [{asset}]` read under RLS).
  - The question has Remove (filled red: the final step) and Cancel. Escape cancels.
  - Remove deletes the row, then the file. A cover that used the picture shows "Picture missing" (C15, a Must fix).
  - When the count or the remove fails, the question says so.
- **N8, the orphan-file sweep:** once per page, when 09 opens, and never when offline. Files under `<user>/assets/` with no `cover_assets` row and older than one hour are removed through the Storage API, 100 a call. Each safety rule below has a test:
  - The full row list is read first. If that read fails, nothing is removed.
  - If the list has 1,000 rows or more, the sweep stays out, because the API may cut the list.
  - The folder is read 100 at a time, at most 50 pages. A failed page removes nothing.
  - Only `<uuid>.jpg|png` names are touched. E15's `<book>/<id>.jpg` files are in another folder and are never touched.
  - A file's age comes from its server time, compared with the server's `now`. Tested with the device clock 3 h ahead: a file 30 minutes old stays.

**Data calls** (`js/supabase.js`): `recordCoverExports` (one insert), `coverAssetUse` and `sweepCoverAssets`. **Scripts:** `app/book.html` now loads `typst.js`, `zip.js`, `export.js` and `export-dialog.js`. **CSS:** `cover.css` Part 10 block, with tokens only.

## 5. Safari (short pass)

`tests/browser/cover/safari-p10.mjs` uses real Safari 27.0.1 with real WebDriver clicks. Every Supabase call is answered in the page, and none reached the host. The engine and fonts load from the real CDNs, as the app does.

**What a passing run covers (25 checks):**
- Export cover opens the dialog: ready, focus on the first option.
- Export finishes as done, and the 11 KDP checks pass in Safari.
- PNG 3712 × 2775 (header read back); Kindle 1600 × 2560, plain sRGB.
- One insert of 3 rows; My layouts saved; the header shows Done and Next is on.
- **Download and Download all (.zip)**, caught at the link's click so no Safari download prompt shows. The zip is read back in Safari: the signatures and the CRC-32 of all 3 files match.
- A JPG export (Safari's own JPEG writer, 3712 × 2775) with Kindle off: 2 rows.
- A real click on Stop: closed, nothing recorded, not done.

**The honest count: 14 runs in all.**
- **Passed (3 runs, 25 / 0; the export took 7.5 s, 30 s and 25 s):**
  - Only the last of the three ran the final script with real clicks only. That run passed every check.
  - The first two still had a debug fallback. If the first click on Export cover gave no dialog, the fallback opened the dialog by script. Every later click in those runs was real.
- **Failed at the first click (10 runs):** the real click on Export cover opened nothing. Three of these were early runs; the last 7 came after the final review. The last 5 were debugged:
  - No pointer, mouse or click event reached the page at all, from the element click or from WebDriver pointer actions.
  - A click on the Checks chip, which worked at STOP 9, gave no event either.
  - In the last run, `document.hasFocus()` was false: the Safari window did not have the focus. Another app was likely in front during that time.
  - So the cause is the environment, not the export. It also fits the unexplained STOP 9 note "the first chip click did nothing".
- **Timed out (1 run):** the export did not finish within 240 s. This was most likely the first, cold download of the Typst engine from the real CDN.
- **The script now checks for this first.** It clicks the Checks chip; if no event arrives, it stops at once with "Safari takes no real input (window focus false)… Bring the Safari window to the front and run again."
